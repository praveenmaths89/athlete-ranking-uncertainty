## Rules used

- Only legal sources: publisher open-access PDFs, PubMed Central open-access subset, arXiv/HAL/NBER preprints, institutional repositories, official CRAN manuals. No shadow libraries, no subscription or login downloads.
- This repository is **public**, so only openly licensed copies (Creative Commons, or GPL for R manuals) are stored here. Free-to-read copies without such a licence are linked, not copied.
- Commercial books are never copied; the table gives DOI and ISBN.
- Preprints and accepted manuscripts can differ from the published version. Cite the published version (DOI).
- Every PDF was checked: it opens as a valid PDF, and the title on its first pages matches the cited work.
